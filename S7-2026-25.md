# Partial Letter

I think Chives is working on something through a more traditional word editor, based on the lack of recent activity here from what I can see. I haven't touched the WhyDRS comments repo outside of drafting independently for TAR. Sorry if that has left things a little messy with three old notes PRs open for CAT.

This rule is really a big deal,[^2] and I think it'd be completely reasonable to add an entry or so on a letter for this. I have been so stunned at how definitive proxy statements required full-set delivery for this long. And the old postcard, while it had its benefits, seemed like government-defined corporate disclosure in the worst centralizing way, ignoring the local requirements of States.[moot point]

The discussion on changing physical delivery is also really interesting. They plainly admit it would help lower costs, but cite the implementation complexity that would arise in disclosing and charging fees. All this to support investors accessing reports "in the form they prefer," which might be paper.

Basically, the issuer inevitably bears the delivery costs (and then some), since intermediaries get to pass them down the custodian chain and out DTC.

At 20, they note how the asset manager committee recommended digital delivery based on "operational challenges that arose at the onset of the COVID-19 pandemic."

I haven't gone through the full 200 pages of substance, but getting rid of the postcard makes this question very important in my eyes.
