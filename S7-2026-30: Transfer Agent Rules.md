Starting thread for work on SECs Proposal to Modernize Transfer Agent Rules

This is exciting! It is the first time in decades that the TA regulations are being seriously considered for meaningful updates, and there have been myriad developments in the technological landscape which warrant this process taking place.

The rules document is hundreds pages with hundreds of suggestions from the SEC. Each one deserves attention and response from the community. Good thing it's a holiday weekend coming up as I write this.

Thoughts on process - Considering the massive size of the proposal document and that they already have it sectioned out into sectioned considerations by sector and legacy rule to be adjusted, we could follow along with them. One thing worth noting is that the Commission will accept comments on any of the language within the body of the document - it does not specifically need to be one of the many questions which are directly asked of the public. The majority of the body of text is laying out their current perceptions as to problems / shortcomings / inefficiencies with the current regulations along with suggestions.
